Projects

Stability Landscape Analysis of Hardy–Weinberg Equilibrium in Evolving Populations

11th March 2026 – Present

We aim to develop a model that quantifies a population’s buffer capacity, as opposed to the simple binary check used to verify if a given population follows the HWE. Essentially, we want to determine how much a population can withstand specific stressors such as genetic drift, migration rates, or non-random mating before it shifts into a different evolutionary state. The model will perform a sensitivity analysis on population data, predicting both the "distance" to a state change and the primary drivers to that instability.
